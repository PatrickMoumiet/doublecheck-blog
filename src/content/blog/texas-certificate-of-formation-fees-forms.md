*Forming a Texas entity starts with a $300 Certificate of Formation. Here are the forms and other fees on the state's schedule.*

**Key takeaways:**

- The Texas certificate of formation for a corporation or LLC costs $300 per the Secretary of State's fee schedule.
- Form 201 is for for-profit corporations and Form 205 is for LLCs.
- Expedited processing is $25 per document, preclearance is $50, and name reservation is $40 for 120 days.
- Franchise tax comes after formation; plan for it.


![Texas filing fees at a glance](/images/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/fees.webp)


## 1. What you file

To form a Texas corporation or LLC you file a **Certificate of Formation** with the Texas Secretary of State. The Secretary of State's fee schedule lists the certificate of formation for a corporation or LLC at **$300**, covering Forms 201 (for-profit corporation) and 205 (LLC), among others.

Context on choosing between entity types is in [LLC vs. C corp for startups](/blog/llc-vs-c-corp-startups/).

## 2. The fee schedule

![Texas fees: $300 certificate of formation, $25 expedited processing, $50 preclearance, $40 name reservation for 120 days, $25 assumed name certificate](/images/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/fees.webp)

- Certificate of formation (corporation or LLC): **$300**
- Expedited processing of a document submitted for filing: **$25**
- Preclearance of a filing instrument: **$50**
- Name reservation (120 days) or renewal, Form 501: **$40**
- Assumed name certificate, Form 503: **$25**

Fees change, so verify against the Secretary of State's current schedule before you file.

## 3. Which form

![Form 201 is the certificate of formation for a for-profit corporation; Form 205 is for a limited liability company; each has a $300 filing fee](/images/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/forms.webp)

Use the form that matches your entity. Do not treat the fee schedule as advice on which entity fits you.

## 4. The sequence

![Forming a Texas entity: choose the type, check the name, pick a registered agent, file online, plan for franchise tax](/images/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/steps.webp)

Check name availability and consider the preclearance or name reservation options, choose a registered agent, file, and then plan the annual franchise tax. Read [Texas franchise tax for LLCs and corporations](/blog/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/). Before you pick a name, run the checks in [trademark and business name checks](/blog/trademark-business-name-check/).
