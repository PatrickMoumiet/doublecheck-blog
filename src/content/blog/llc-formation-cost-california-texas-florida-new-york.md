*The filing fee is only part of the cost of an LLC. Here is how four big states compare.*

**Key takeaways:**

- California charges an $800 annual tax; Texas $300 to file plus franchise tax reports; Florida $100 plus $25; New York $50 plus publication.
- Recurring and state-specific costs often exceed the filing fee.
- Delaware corporations owe a franchise tax with $175 or $400 minimums.
- Verify every number on the state's website before you file.


![LLC formation costs by state](/images/llc-formation-cost-california-texas-florida-new-york/by-state.webp)


## 1. Why the filing fee misleads

The headline filing fee is rarely your real cost. Each state adds different recurring or one-time items, so compare the whole picture. This article lines up facts from official or reliable sources for four large states and points to deeper guides.

## 2. What each state charges

![What each state charges: California $800 annual tax, Texas $300 certificate of formation, Florida $100 plus $25 agent designation, New York publication plus $50, Delaware corporation $175 or $400 minimum](/images/llc-formation-cost-california-texas-florida-new-york/by-state.webp)

- **California:** $800 annual tax for every LLC until it cancels, plus an LLC fee above $250,000 in income ([details](/blog/california-llc-annual-tax-and-fee/)).
- **Texas:** $300 certificate of formation, then franchise tax reports due May 15 ([forms and fees](/blog/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/); [franchise tax](/blog/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/)).
- **Florida:** $100 for Articles of Organization and $25 for registered agent designation, per the Florida Division of Corporations. Annual reports are due between January 1 and May 1 of the year after formation, and failure to file leads to administrative dissolution.
- **New York:** $50 Certificate of Publication plus publication costs of roughly $600 to $2,000 ([publication rules](/blog/new-york-llc-publication-requirement/)).
- **Delaware corporation:** $175 or $400 minimum annual franchise tax ([details](/blog/delaware-franchise-tax-startups/)).

## FAQ

**How much does it cost to form an LLC in Florida?**

The Division of Corporations lists $100 for Articles of Organization and $25 for registered agent designation.

**How much is an LLC in Texas?**

$300 for the certificate of formation per the Secretary of State fee schedule, plus any franchise tax reporting.

**Which state has the highest ongoing LLC cost?**

That depends on your income and facts. California's $800 annual tax applies every year, and New York requires paid publication at formation.

**Do fees change?**

Yes. Verify against each state's official website.
