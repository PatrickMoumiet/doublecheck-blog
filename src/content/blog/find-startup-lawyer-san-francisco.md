*Finding a lawyer in San Francisco is easier when you know what to ask. Here is a practical process.*

**Key takeaways:**

- The Bar Association of San Francisco runs the SF-Marin Lawyer Referral Service, certified by the State Bar.
- Participating attorneys must carry malpractice insurance and be in good standing.
- Verify any lawyer's State Bar license yourself.
- Prepare questions on scope, fees, and who does the work.

## 1. Start with what you need

Before you search, decide what you need help with: a contract, equity, a dispute, or a financing. See [when to hire a lawyer](/blog/when-to-hire-lawyer-small-business/) and [how to choose a good lawyer](/blog/how-to-choose-good-lawyer/).

## 2. The SF-Marin referral service

![SF-Marin Lawyer Referral Service: run by the Bar Association of San Francisco, certified by the State Bar, screens attorneys, receives about 50,000 inquiries a year](/images/find-startup-lawyer-san-francisco/lris.webp)

The Bar Association of San Francisco runs the **SF-Marin Lawyer Referral and Information Service (LRIS)**. According to the Bar Association:

- LRIS is certified by the State Bar of California and regulated under rules of the California Supreme Court.
- Participating attorneys must have malpractice insurance and be in good standing with the State Bar.
- LRIS also uses objective experience standards and performs ongoing reviews of attorney performance.
- It receives approximately 50,000 inquiries each year from San Francisco and Marin residents and businesses.

A referral service is a starting point, not an endorsement of any specific lawyer. For more on how referrals work, see [how to get a lawyer referral](/blog/how-to-get-lawyer-referral/).

## 3. A simple process

![Finding a lawyer in San Francisco: define the need, ask founders and advisers, use the bar referral service, verify the State Bar license, discuss scope and cost](/images/find-startup-lawyer-san-francisco/steps.webp)

Ask founders and advisers you trust, try the referral service, then verify each candidate's license ([how to verify a California attorney](/blog/verify-california-attorney-license-state-bar/)) and discuss scope and cost before engaging.

## 4. Seven questions for the first call

![Seven questions for your first call: have you handled this, who works on my matter, how do you charge, what is the scope, how fast will you reply, what do you need from me, can I bring my AI draft](/images/find-startup-lawyer-san-francisco/questions.webp)

Bring your documents. If you used AI to draft something, say so and bring it; see [lawyer review of a ChatGPT contract](/blog/lawyer-review-chatgpt-contract/) and [how to know if your lawyer is good](/blog/how-do-i-know-if-my-lawyer-is-good/).
