*Texas has a franchise tax on many businesses. Here is who files, the rates, and the deadline.*

**Key takeaways:**

- The Texas franchise tax report is due May 15 each year.
- Rates are 0.375% for retail or wholesale and 0.75% for other businesses.
- Zenind reports a $2,650,000 no tax due threshold for reports due in 2026 and 2027, but entities below it may still need to file.
- Sole proprietorships are generally not taxable entities.


![Texas franchise tax numbers](/images/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/numbers.webp)


## 1. What it is

Texas has no personal income tax but it does have a **franchise tax** on many business entities. Per a CPA Practice Advisor summary, it applies to taxable entities formed in or doing business in Texas, including LLCs, corporations, partnerships, S corporations, and trusts; out-of-state businesses can owe it too.

Formation basics are in [Texas certificate of formation fees and forms](/blog/texas-certificate-of-formation-fees-forms/).

## 2. Who is included

![Texas franchise tax: most business entities, S corporations, trusts and out-of-state businesses are included; sole proprietorships and certain nonprofits are generally exempt](/images/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/who-files.webp)

Sole proprietorships are generally not taxable entities (the summary notes an exception for single-member LLCs), as are certain trusts, estates, and nonprofits. Your exact status depends on your entity and activities, so confirm with the Comptroller or an accountant.

## 3. The numbers

![Texas franchise tax: due May 15; no tax due threshold of $2,650,000 for reports due in 2026 and 2027; 0.375% rate for retail or wholesale, 0.75% for others](/images/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/numbers.webp)

- **Due date:** May 15 each year, or the next business day if it falls on a weekend or holiday.
- **Rates:** 0.375% for retail or wholesale businesses and 0.75% for other businesses.
- **No tax due threshold:** Zenind's guide reports $2,650,000 for reports due in 2026 and 2027. Entities below it owe no tax for that period, but may still need to file a report.
- **Taxable margin:** generally the greater of 70% of total revenue, revenue minus cost of goods sold, revenue minus compensation, or revenue minus $1 million.

Thresholds change by period, so confirm the number for your report year.

## 4. Your yearly routine

![Texas franchise tax routine: track revenue, compare to the threshold, find the right report, file by May 15, respond to notices](/images/texas-franchise-tax-llc-corporation/routine.webp)

Track annualized revenue, compare it to the threshold, identify the correct report, and file by May 15. Ask your accountant to own the date.

## FAQ

**When is the Texas franchise tax due?**

May 15, or the next business day if it falls on a weekend or holiday.

**What is the Texas franchise tax rate?**

0.375% for retail or wholesale businesses and 0.75% for others, per a CPA Practice Advisor summary.

**Do small LLCs owe Texas franchise tax?**

Entities below the no tax due threshold owe no tax for the period, but may still need to file a report.

**Are sole proprietorships subject to it?**

Generally no, though the summary notes an exception for single-member LLCs. Confirm with the Comptroller.
