*A two-minute check before you hire. Here is how to verify a California lawyer with the State Bar.*

**Key takeaways:**

- The State Bar of California offers an Attorney Licensee Search.
- Compare the profile's status and details to what the lawyer told you.
- If something does not match, ask before you engage.
- Verify before sharing sensitive documents or paying a retainer.


![What to check on an attorney profile](/images/verify-california-attorney-license-state-bar/what-to-check.webp)


## 1. Why verify

Hiring a lawyer starts with confirming that the person is licensed. The State Bar of California maintains an **Attorney Licensee Search** where you can look up a lawyer's profile. This takes minutes and should happen before you share sensitive documents or pay a retainer.

Pair this with [how to choose a good lawyer](/blog/how-to-choose-good-lawyer/).

## 2. What to check

![What to check on an attorney profile: license status, any discipline listed, status notes, name and bar number, practice area, contact details](/images/verify-california-attorney-license-state-bar/what-to-check.webp)

Look up the lawyer by name or bar number and compare the profile to what you were told: license status, whether any discipline or status notes are listed, and whether the name and contact details match. We have not reproduced the profile's exact field names; read what the page shows.

## 3. A quick flow

![Verification flow: get the name and bar number, search the State Bar licensee search, read status and history, compare to what the lawyer told you, then decide on scope and cost](/images/verify-california-attorney-license-state-bar/flow.webp)

Get the name and bar number, search, read the profile, and compare it with what you were told. Then decide scope and cost.

## 4. When to pause

![When to pause: something does not match, ask the lawyer to clear it up, then decide to choose another lawyer or proceed informed](/images/verify-california-attorney-license-state-bar/red-flags.webp)

If something does not match, ask. If the answer does not satisfy you, choose someone else. See [how to know if your lawyer is good](/blog/how-do-i-know-if-my-lawyer-is-good/).

## 5. Referral services

Referral services add a layer of screening. The Bar Association of San Francisco says its referral service requires malpractice insurance and good standing with the State Bar; see [finding a startup lawyer in San Francisco](/blog/find-startup-lawyer-san-francisco/) and [how to get a lawyer referral](/blog/how-to-get-lawyer-referral/). You can still verify each lawyer yourself.

## FAQ

**How do I check if a California lawyer is licensed?**

Use the State Bar of California's Attorney Licensee Search, by name or bar number.

**What should I compare?**

License status, any listed discipline or status notes, and whether name and contact details match what you were told.

**Should I verify a lawyer from a referral service?**

Referral services add screening, but you can still verify each lawyer yourself.

**Does a license mean the lawyer is right for my matter?**

No. Also check experience with your issue, scope, and fees.
